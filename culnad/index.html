About

CULNAD Construction Company Limited

CULNAD LLC is a medium construction company that has successfully operated in Ghana for the past years, working on both small and large scale construction, repair and alteration projects focusing on residential, commercial and industrial contracting.

In the early years we were a company of few men working hard to satisfy our clients. Over the times, we have evolved into a serious result-oriented company with the injection of professionalism and a competitive pricing policy, without sacrificing quality, to enable our clients achieve maximum satisfaction.

Our team of professionals brings to bear their passion, focus and experience in every task undertaken. So today, we are privileged to provide exceptional service to a more diverse community across Ghana.

Our association with satisfied clients like Danwill Company, Great Majesty, Jodels Company and South Belfast Ltd. is further manifestation of our dedication to excellence.

Facts

Who we are

Our Name and Logo

The name of the company is CULNAD Construction Company Limited.

Our logo consists of three "C" alphabets and a triangle arranged in a square loop. Inserted are a hand trowel, a chisel hammer, a try square and the Map of Africa. These identify us as construction constructors of African origin.

The blend of gold and white herald company ethos and philosophy of illumination, passion, courage, safety, brilliance, possibility, sincerity, wisdom and perfection. The Logo, the Name and the "Gold/White" colors are trademarks of CULNAD LLC.

Vision

To be sought out as the recognized and trusted firm in the industry and global community; retaining, developing and attracting the best in committed construction leadership; and to drive deliberate and intelligent growth of the company.

Mission

  • To perform for our customers the highest level of quality construction services at fair and market competitive prices.
  • To continually improve the competence of our team, and employ diverse innovative and results oriented personnel, motivated to deliver excellence.
  • To maintain the highest levels of professionalism, integrity, honesty and fairness in our relationships with our suppliers, sub-contractors, professional associates and customers.
  • To ensure the longevity of our company through repeat and referral business achieved by customer satisfaction in all areas including timelines, attention to detail and service-minded attitudes.

Health & Safety

CULNAD is committed to providing work places which are safe, healthy and friendly environments where safe working practices are insisted upon. We know that the health and safety culture comes from the top of the company, therefore our management is committed to:

  • Providing a safe place of work.
  • Providing safe system of work.
  • Providing training and instruction.
  • Providing and maintaining safe equipment.
  • Assessing the risk to anyone who might be affected by carrying out work activities.
  • Ensuring materials and substances are safely stored, handled and transported.

Our Partners

Alpha partners. Our employees are our Alpha partners. They are at the heart of CULNAD for we believe in treating them with respect and dignity. We are proud to offer incentives and packages for our partners, including health coverage for eligible and full- and part-time partners, access to credit facilities and Trade and Industry Standards Training.

Beta partners. Our Beta Partners are reliable large-scale businesses with proven track record. Our shared bond has contributed to the excellence at CULNAD. We have together worked hard to forge and maintain a long history of successes and smiles.

Working process

How we work

Step 01

Initial meeting

We meet the project owner or representative to discuss project description, process, objectives, timeline, corporate identity and budget in broad outline.

Step 02

Research and concepts

Through research and brainstorming, concepts and designs are developed together with their estimated costs.

Step 03

Review and feedback

Designs are reviewed and critiqued to select the most appropriate one. You evaluate and give feedback.

Step 04

Build, with room to change

We stick to industry standards, and still make room for you to make changes while the project is ongoing.
